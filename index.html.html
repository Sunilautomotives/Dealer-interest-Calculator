Settings

Calculation rules
  • End date = Sales Date (or Sales Date − 1 day if "Exclude sale date" is chosen). If Sales Date is blank (vehicle in stock), the As on date is used.
  • Days = Allocate Date to New Sale Date, both days included. Only days inside the selected period are counted, split by month.
  • Vehicle Price = the price in the CSV if given; otherwise the Urbane price if the model contains the Urbane keyword, else the Default price.
  • Interest for each month = (Vehicle Price × Rate% ÷ 30) × Days in that month. Default 1.5% per month, same as the sheet formula (($J × 0.015) / 30) × Days. (Per day: Price × Rate% × Days; Per year: Price × Rate% ÷ 365 × Days.)
  • Example: Allotment 01-10-2026, Sale 08-10-2026, Price ₹1,00,000, 1.5% per month → (₹1,00,000 × 0.015 ÷ 30) × 8 = ₹50 × 8 = ₹400. Urbane ₹1,20,000 → ₹60 × 8 = ₹480.
  • Across months: Allotment 18-06-2025, Sale 09-07-2025 → June 13 days + July 9 days = 22 days, each month charged separately.
